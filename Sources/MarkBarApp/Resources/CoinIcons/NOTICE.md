The SVG strings were statically extracted from `package/dist/svgs/tokens/{background,mono}/*.svg.js` without executing package code. 1,788 background and 1,544 mono assets are included unmodified. The colored background variants are clipped to circles by the shared renderer for consistent contrast on light and dark surfaces. There are no external image or script references. The application loads only local bundle assets and caches images by symbol, variant and size.

Lookup is exact by the complete symbol; numeric prefixes are never removed or aliased. Assets are decorative and do not determine contract identity, price units or market data routing. Missing assets use a neutral text badge beside the complete symbol. Compact menu-bar rows fall back to the complete symbol when no mono asset exists. For example, this snapshot has no 0G, 2Z or 1000PEPE asset; A is Vaulta, 1INCH is 1inch.
